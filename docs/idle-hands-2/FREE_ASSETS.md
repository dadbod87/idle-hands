# Free-assets and operating-cost policy

User constraint, 2026-11-09: keep the game free if possible and do not pay for assets. Use original code/SVG/placeholder shapes or verified CC0 packs first. Free assets are a ceiling of $0 purchases, not a request to spend a small amount.

## Before importing anything

Record stable asset ID, creator, source URL, exact archive/version/hash and file list, included license text, modification notes, intended use and required credit in a versioned manifest. Preserve notices in the sequel's licenses directory and expose credits in the game. Engine/tool licenses do not clear bundled artwork, fonts, music, demo maps or character sprites.

Kenney's asset pages are CC0 according to its [official terms](https://kenney.nl/support). Tiny Town is the first sample candidate, not a complete acquired art set. Inspect the actual archive before adding files. Give optional creator credit and never use the Kenney logo. Missing animation/interior coverage is work to plan, not permission to mix incompatible packs.

Paid packs, paid editors/services and marketplace subscriptions are excluded. LimeZu is retained in historical research only and is not eligible under the present constraint. Mixed-license LPC art remains deferred; CC-BY can be used with complete attribution if needed, but no share-alike/restricted/unknown files without a reviewed distribution plan. Internet screenshots, game rips and “free download” mirrors are not sources.

Original assets still need provenance: author/date/source file and any references. Use existing image tools only under their actual terms; do not assume AI-generated art or a third-party API has zero cost. No runtime AI/image service, telemetry, analytics or asset CDN dependency by default. System fonts and local assets keep the prototype simple.

## Hosting clarification (2026-11-09)

Nick prefers GitHub/Vercel/Supabase and is open to modest business-funded extra hosting. No exact recurring budget or provider purchase is authorized yet. Prefer appropriate existing platform experience while creating separate sequel resources when approved; do not reuse real 1.0 data. A small, justified hosting bill may be acceptable after presenting the actual estimate. The asset-purchase budget remains $0.

## Hosting is a separate budget

Open-source engines have no engine purchase fee under their licenses; servers, database, bandwidth, email and backups may still cost money. Local development uses existing tools and no new cloud resources. At IH2-060 measure 20-client memory/CPU, connection-hours, stored data, transfer and query rates. Evaluate free-tier terms, sleeping/connection limits, backup/restore and abuse controls against actual play. Do not sign up for a trial that can silently become paid or attach billing without approval. If zero-cost reliable hosting is not feasible, present a concrete reduced-scope option and an optional cost estimate before spending. Public beta is gated on this decision.

No art imported, paid product installed, new hosted service created or charge incurred by this planning task.
