## Initial pacing assessment

The original nine/fifteen weekly-stamp targets could be completed in one session and are superseded. Seven coordinated players can contribute at most 21 on-theme credits on one Pacific date and 42 across a midnight crossing, but the four-date requirement still prevents a twelve-hour finish. Three contributors doing all three categories on four dates can supply the 36-credit base; the five-person, six-date stretch requires 60 credits. From a normal Friday 16:00 reset the earliest possible base is Monday 00:00 and the earliest stretch Wednesday 00:00, assuming play across the weekend. Office-day-only participation can finish later. Dates need not be consecutive, nobody must log in every day, and a late casual participant still receives the earned memento after contributing. Existing two/four/six-week personal collections are unchanged.

The cupboard restores at most three charges total per player per week, not three for every tool. It can raise one tool from two to five uses over a week only if all earned refills go to that tool; remaining charges never exceed two. Shields add a continuing BW purchase rather than infinite passive protection. With the current catalog, the most expensive base sabotage is thirty minutes of base production, making the shield one hour at the current-week retained benchmark (900 BW at its 0.25/sec floor). Live boosted/discounted attack prices can differ. Two simultaneous attackers consume one shield and leave one attack to resolve normally against any other equipped/passive defense.
