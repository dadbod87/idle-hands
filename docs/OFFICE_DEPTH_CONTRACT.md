## Initial pacing assessment

Two dominant players can supply at most six counted project stamps; they cannot finish the nine-stamp/three-person base alone. Three contributors completing the three listed categories can finish the base; five can earn the optional fifteen-stamp stretch. One on-theme contribution earns a memento once the office finishes, so later casual contributors are not locked out. The individual collection still needs two active Pacific dates and work/variety; wealth cannot buy weeks.

The cupboard restores at most three charges total per player per week, not three for every tool. It can raise one tool from two to five uses over a week only if all earned refills go to that tool; remaining charges never exceed two. Shields add a continuing BW purchase rather than infinite passive protection. With the current catalog, the most expensive base sabotage is thirty minutes of base production, making the shield one hour at the current-week retained benchmark (900 BW at its 0.25/sec floor). Live boosted/discounted attack prices can differ. Two simultaneous attackers consume one shield and leave one attack to resolve normally against any other equipped/passive defense.

These are mathematical/isolated-fixture pacing checks, not human playtest or retention evidence. Inspect the exact-head browser results and keep physical-phone/hosted-backend limitations explicit before release. An unused weekly shield expires by week key; restructuring cannot restore it or tool charges.

Shield pricing uses a separate private peak keyed by player and Pacific week. It survives restructures within that week but never inherits a lifetime furniture-price peak or a previous week’s rate. This avoids carrying last week’s defensive purchase costs into a fresh Friday economy. Permanent furniture pricing keeps its lifetime benchmark.
